# Juleb

> Juleb is a Saudi Arabia-based pharma-tech and retail software company offering an integrated cloud platform for the health, retail, and distribution sectors. It covers accounting, inventory and warehouse management, sales force automation, point of sale, Rx e-prescription, e-commerce, and DSCSA/regulatory compliance, with connectors for ZATCA/Fatoora, RSD, GS1, Wasfaty, Salla, Shopify, HungerStation, Geidea, Alhamrani, and Microsoft Dynamics. Backed by 500 Global.
